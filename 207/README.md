# Flip & Find

A relaxing, browser-based memory card game. Flip cards to find matching pairs, and try to finish in as few moves as possible.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a modern web browser. No installation or build step is needed.

## How to play

1. Choose a difficulty.
2. Flip two cards to look for a matching pair.
3. Find every pair to finish the game.
4. Use **New game** or **Play again** to reshuffle and start over.

Your best move count is saved in your browser separately for each difficulty.

## Difficulties

| Difficulty | Board | Pairs |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Easy | 4 × 4 | 8 |
| Medium | 4 × 5 | 10 |
| Hard | 6 × 6 | 18 |

## Project files

- `index.html` — game page and interface
- `styles.css` — layout, responsive styling, and card animations
- `game.js` — card shuffling, game rules, timer, and saved best scores
